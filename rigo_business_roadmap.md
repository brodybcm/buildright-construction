## Phase 3 — LLC Formation

**Steps:**
1. Choose the state of operation
2. Check business name availability with the Secretary of State
3. File Articles of Organization (~\$50–\$500 depending on state)
4. Get an EIN (free via IRS.gov)
5. Open a dedicated business checking account
6. Optional: Registered Agent service

---

## Phase 4 — Google Business Profile

- Claim/create at business.google.com
- Add: Business name, address (or service area), phone, hours, photos, services
- Encourage first reviews from existing happy customers (10 years of word-of-mouth = gold here)
